names were changed to the emoji's code points.

- 👍 1f44d, ❤️ 2764_fe0f, 😂 1f602, 😮 1f62e, 😢 1f622, 🙏 1f64f: taken over from the retired client's
  `assets/reactions/` (tellomi/tellomi ADR-0059).
- 👎 1f44e, 🔥 1f525, 🎉 1f389, 👏 1f44f: downloaded 2026-10-17 from
  `https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/e/notoemoji/latest/<code point>/lottie.json`.

The attribution is also listed in Settings.bundle/Acknowledgements.plist, which the app's "Open source licenses" page
and the system Settings app show. They are not Telegram's animations.
